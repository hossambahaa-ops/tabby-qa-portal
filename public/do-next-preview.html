How it works
  • Bell badge reflects unseen items only — once you open it, the pulse animation calms. Counts split by tab so System chatter doesn't drown out human signals.
  • "Do this next" panel renders on every page, not just Dashboard. Items are role-filtered: leads see acks/AP detections, admins see system health alerts, QAs see their own pending tasks.
  • Click-through takes you straight to the relevant record (no landing on Dashboard then re-searching).
  • Empty state ("You're all caught up 🎉") is its own little reward — meaningful for a workspace this busy.
