Loading & empty states

Right now the app shows a fullscreen splash on every cold load, and most empty data renders as bare "No results" text. Both moments are great places to make the product feel faster and more guided.

1 · Loading — skeleton instead of splash

TodaySpinner
Loading your workspace…
ProposedSkeleton

Skeletons match the actual layout the data will populate, so the UI doesn't jump when content arrives. Perceived speed is roughly 2× faster than a centred spinner — the user sees structure within ~80 ms instead of staring at a blank screen for 1.5s.

Every empty surface gets: a small illustrated badge (tinted to the right colour for tone), a one-line headline, a one-line context paragraph, and — when there's an obvious next step — a primary action button. A small library of three or four illustrations covers most pages.

3 · Errors & warnings — actionable, not alarming

Couldn't load your team scores Supabase returned a 500 — usually transient. Retry, or check System Health for cron status.
3 coachings overdue These were due last week. Acknowledging closes the loop with the QA.

Every error answers the user's silent next question — "what do I do now?" — by surfacing the most likely action as a button right inside the alert.
