For senior_qa and above:
  • Full month visible at once (31 day columns). No expander needed.
  • Compact 40 × 40 px cells — about 50 % smaller than the standard lead view.
  • QA name column is sticky-left so the row label stays visible as you scroll horizontally.
  • Top stat strip surfaces month-level totals (P / NSNC / pending / AL / adherence) for fast scanning.
  • Cells show only the status badge — no check-in time inline (too dense). Hover any cell to pop a tooltip with the full detail (time, plan, lead override notes).
  • Click any cell to edit (same picker as today).
  • Today's column outlined in green across every QA row.

Pure QAs and regular leads stay on the week-first view (the previous preview). This compact full-month layout only kicks in at senior_qa and above.
