For qa_lead and above:
  • Full month visible at once (31 day columns). No expander needed.
  • Each cell is ~54 × 58 px and shows three things in one glance:
    • Plan badge (top-right) — P or H, what was scheduled.
    • Status badge (middle) — P / NSNC / AL / ⏳ / H — what actually happened.
    • Check-in time (bottom) — 09:14, auto, window — when the QA checked in.
  • Plan ≠ status is instantly visible: a cell with plan badge "P" but body colour "NSNC" red, or plan "P" but body "AL" blue, shows the mismatch in one glance.
  • Sticky-left QA name column stays visible while scrolling horizontally.
  • Today's column outlined in green across every QA row.
  • Click any cell to edit (same picker as today). Hover for richer tooltip.

QA / senior_qa still use the week-first own-calendar view. qa_lead and above get this compact full-month team view.
