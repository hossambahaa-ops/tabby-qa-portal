Tabby Pulse — QA-facing schedule

Three places the QA can see their plan + their check-in trail. Each one keeps the check-in time visible alongside the actual status. Pick whichever fits how QAs would naturally land on the info.

A · Week strip on Dashboard B · Dedicated month grid C · Profile attendance tab

A · Week strip on Dashboard

Lives on the Dashboard, top
A compact 7-day rolling strip on the QA's dashboard. Today is highlighted in green. Each day shows plan (P / H / OFF) and actual outcome with the check-in time. Click any day to drill in. Cheapest to build — just one extra card on the dashboard.

B · Dedicated month grid page

New "My Schedule" link in the sidebar
A full month grid view (like Apple/Google calendar). Each cell colour-coded by status. Hover/tap any cell to see check-in time. Toolbar to switch months. This is the most "real" calendar experience and gives the QA full visibility forward and backward.

C · Attendance tab on the Profile page

Folded into existing QA Profile
Adds an "Attendance" tab to the existing QA Profile page. The QA sees their own; their lead sees theirs. A simple chronological list — date, plan, actual, check-in time, any lead notes. No new pages or nav items.
