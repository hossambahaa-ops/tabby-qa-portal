L1 · Today's huddle card on the Lead dashboard

Daily monitoring · most actionable
A live "who's where right now" card. Lists every direct report, sorted with no-shows first, then pending, then present. Each row shows plan, current actual status, check-in time, and inline buttons to nudge or override. Refreshes automatically as QAs check in.

L2 · Team week grid

Planning + history view
The whole team as rows, days of the week as columns. Each cell colour-coded by status, with check-in time inside. Lead sees patterns: who skips Tuesdays, who's always late, who's solid. Click any cell to override. Works for past and future weeks.
